# Playing

PhunTaxi replaces the spawn list with places you have found. You start
knowing a few. Everything else you find by going there, and what you find is
kept for your **next** character, not just this one.

## Waking up

A new character wakes up in the **Taxi Garage**, a walled room off the map.
There is no door out. A line pulses over the taxi: right click it and choose
**Take taxi ride**.

The window that opens lists the places you know, grouped by town, with a map
beside them. Click a town to open it and fit the map to it, pick a place, and
press **Go**. The ride is free, and you only get one: once you have chosen,
that is where you start.

If you close the window by mistake, walk back to the taxi and open it again.

Where you can go is everything **your player** has found, over every
character you have played in this world. A brand new player knows only the
start points the server gives them.

> If the server has **Wake Up Indoors** off, or the garage is full, you skip
> the garage and are put straight at the last place you chose, or a start
> point you know.

## Finding places

There are two kinds of spawn point.

**Places** are found by walking there. Get within a dozen squares or so
(the server decides) and stay a moment, and you get "You will remember this
place". The check runs about once a game minute, so sprinting past does not
always count.

If the server shows undiscovered places, they are in your list greyed out, as
somewhere to head for.

**Pay phones** are the other kind, and the one that matters most. Some of the
pay phones in the world are live. To find one, stand at it, right click, and
choose **Pick up the phone**. A live phone gives you "You hear heavy
breathing" and you will remember it. Once you know a phone, the option is no
longer offered on it.

A live phone you have not found yet **rings** now and then while you are
nearby. Follow the sound.

Pay phones are never shown in your list until you have used them. Live ones
cannot be picked up, moved, rotated, dismantled or smashed.

## Dying

Your unlocks are kept with your player, not with the character. When a
character dies, the next one wakes up in the Taxi Garage and can take the
free ride to any place you have ever found. Heading far out without finding
a pay phone raises the stakes; finding one lowers them.

What you have found belongs to this world. If the server is wiped, your
unlocks go with it.

## The taxi between phones

If the server has it on, right click a pay phone you know and choose **Call
a taxi**. The window lists the other pay phones you know, with the fare on the
Go button. The places everybody knows from the start come with a pay phone of
their own, right where you arrive, and you know it already. The fare is paid from your PhunMart change, and is taken only once
the ride has gone ahead.

A taxi will not come:

- from a phone you have not picked up yet: pick it up first,
- while a zombie is close (six squares by default),
- to the phone you are already standing at,
- if you cannot afford it, or
- into somebody else's safehouse.

## Safehouses

Nobody is ever taken into somebody else's safehouse, by the first ride or by
a taxi. If the place you picked is inside one, you are told so, and on the
first ride you keep your free choice. Members of a safehouse can ride home.

## Building your own pay phone

If the server allows it, you can put up pay phones of your own.

1. Craft a **Pay Phone Kit** (Electrical, needs Electricity 4): a screwdriver,
   5 electronics scrap, 2 electric wire, a sheet of metal and 4 screws.
2. With the kit on you, stand where a traveller should arrive, right click the
   square **beside** you and choose **Install pay phone**. The phone goes on
   that square, facing you.

It has to go:

- somewhere both the phone's square and the one in front of it are open floor,
- at least 50 squares from any other live pay phone (the server decides),
- not in somebody else's safehouse. Indoors and your own safehouse are fine.

You can have 5 standing at once, unless the server says otherwise. If it is
refused, you keep the kit.

You know your own phone straight away. Everybody else finds it the usual way,
by picking it up. **Take down your pay phone** on it removes it and gives you
the kit back, unless an admin has chosen to keep it.

Built phones belong to the world. A wipe takes them.
